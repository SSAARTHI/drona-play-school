OUR STORY

A Small Beginning, Filled With Big Dreams.

Drona Play School is a small, home-based learning space created with a love for teaching and nurturing young minds.

Guided by P. Sreeja, our approach focuses on personal interaction, creative activities and joyful learning. Every child gets the opportunity to explore, express themselves and learn at their own pace.
